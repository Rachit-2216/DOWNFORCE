DOWNFORCE public data snapshot

DOWNFORCE is an independent, unofficial, non-commercial motorsport research
project. It is not affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA, teams or data providers.
Formula 1 and related names and marks belong to their respective owners.

The compact product-data snapshot contains selected normalized historical race
records, derived analytics, replay records and versioned model artifacts. Its
manifest retains source and content identities. It is not a live data feed or
the complete native telemetry corpus. Missing observations remain unavailable.

Historical archive source: Jolpica F1 and its upstream/community contributors.
